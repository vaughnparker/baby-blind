# 07 - Phase 2A: Constructed Short Memos

## What is Phase 2A?

In Phase 2A, scrambles are constructed so that your total memo — edges and corners
combined — is exactly a specific length. This is different from Phase 1, where the
restriction was on which letters could appear. Here, all 22 edge letters and all 21
corner letters are in play. The only restriction is how many you need to memorize.

Parity never occurs in Phase 2A. You only need the **T Perm** and **Modified Y Perm**.

---

## The Levels

### Level 1: 2 total letters
Your memo will contain exactly 2 letters in total, across edges and corners combined.

> **Note:** With only 2 letters and no parity, the only valid splits are 2 edges and
> 0 corners, or 0 edges and 2 corners. A split of 1 edge and 1 corner would require
> an odd number of edge letters, which means parity — and parity is excluded in this
> level. So every Level 1 scramble is effectively either edges-only or corners-only.

### Level 2: 4 total letters
Your memo will contain exactly 4 letters in total.

### Level 3: 6 total letters
Your memo will contain exactly 6 letters in total.

### Level 4: 8 total letters
Your memo will contain exactly 8 letters in total.

### Level 5: 10 total letters
Your memo will contain exactly 10 letters in total.

### Level 6: 12 total letters
Your memo will contain exactly 12 letters in total.

### Level 7: 14 total letters
Your memo will contain exactly 14 letters in total.

---

## How to Use the Scramble Files

The process is the same as in Phase 1:

1. **Start with a solved cube.**
2. **Apply the scramble** from the file to your cube.
3. **Inspect the cube** and build your memo — edges first, then corners.
4. **Put on your blindfold.**
5. **Solve edges** using the T Perm.
6. **Solve corners** using the Modified Y Perm.
7. **Remove your blindfold** and check if the cube is solved.

Work through the levels in order, starting with
`memo_length_2_scrambles.txt`. Use the **10 successful solves in a row** criterion
before moving to the next level.
