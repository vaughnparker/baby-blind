# 05 - Bridge: From Phase 1 to Phase 2

## Congratulations

You have completed Phase 1. You can now recognize and trace every edge and corner
piece on the cube. This is a significant achievement — most people who attempt
blindfolded solving never get this far.

Take a moment to appreciate what you have built. You have gone from knowing nothing
about blindfolded solving to having a complete mental map of every piece on the cube.

---

## What You Have Accomplished

By completing Phase 1, you have developed two fundamental skills:

1. **Recognition** — you can look at any sticker on the cube and immediately know
   its Speffz letter.

2. **Tracing** — you can follow a cycle of pieces from the buffer, through each
   target, and back to the buffer, building up your memo letter by letter.

These skills are the foundation of everything that follows.

---

## Before Moving to Phase 2

Phase 1 trained edges and corners separately. Before moving on, it is worth spending
some time doing **full scrambles** — scrambles where both edges and corners need to
be solved.

A full scramble will look just like a real blindfolded solve. You will memo all the
edges first, then all the corners, then solve edges, then corners.

Try 10-20 full scrambles before moving to Phase 2. You can generate a random scramble
using any standard Rubik's cube scramble generator. The goal is simply to get
comfortable with the full process before Phase 2 begins adding memo length pressure.

Do not worry about how long it takes. Do not worry about your memo length. Just focus
on completing the solve correctly.

---

## What Phase 2 is About

In Phase 1, the difficulty was controlled by restricting which pieces were scrambled.
In Phase 2, all pieces are in play — but the difficulty is now controlled by the
**length of your memo**.

You will start with very short memos and gradually work up to full-length solves. By
the end of Phase 2, you will be doing complete, unrestricted blindfolded solves.
